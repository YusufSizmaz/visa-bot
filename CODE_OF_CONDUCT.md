# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want VisaBot to be a project people are glad they contributed to. Everyone is welcome here,
regardless of experience level, background, nationality, gender identity, or anything else that
has nothing to do with the code.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about context, not competence.
- Critique code, not people. "This query will lock the table" is useful; "this is amateur work"
  is not.
- Accept that maintainers may say no. A rejected idea is not a rejected person, and a project
  that accepts everything becomes a project nobody can maintain.
- Remember that most people here are writing English as a second language. Read generously.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
messages — and to anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks these rules, contact the maintainers
privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and we will not retaliate against
anyone for reporting in good faith.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block accounts when
this document is violated. We would much rather resolve things with a conversation.
